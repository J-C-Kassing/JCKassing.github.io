**Editors**: H. Bolke-Hermanns, J.-C. Kassing, M. Schaub, and M. Scholkemper

Since the early 2000s, the annual joint meeting of the German Research Training Groups (Graduiertenkollegs) funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG) has been held at Schloss Dagstuhl - Leibniz Centre for Informatics. This volume documents the abstracts of the research topics of the funded researchers in the participating research training groups. The 2024 meeting was organised by RTG 2236 UnRAVeL (Uncertainty and Randomness in Algorithms, Verification, and Logic) and took place from May 26 to May 29, 2024 (Dagstuhl Event 24223).
